# Legal notes: open questions before any real call

Prototype draft. Not legal advice. Every item below is an open question, not a conclusion, and is marked to verify with counsel.

## Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
- What must the notice say, and in which languages, before consent is taken for dose calls? **To verify with counsel.**
- Is consent recorded by a nurse at the desk, with time and name, enough as a record of consent? Does the patient need a copy? **To verify with counsel.**
- Is health information treated differently under the Act or its rules once notified? **To verify with counsel.**
- Who is the data fiduciary: the hospital, the Care Taker operator, or both? Is a processor agreement needed? **To verify with counsel.**
- How long may transcripts be kept after the course ends, and how is erasure handled? **To verify with counsel.**
- Is "Share with family" a separate purpose that needs its own consent? **To verify with counsel.**
- What applies when a family member answers the call instead of the patient? **To verify with counsel.**

## TRAI rules on calls to subscribers
- Are automated dose reminder calls treated as transactional or service calls under the TRAI commercial communication regulations? **To verify with counsel.**
- Is registration of a header or number series needed for automated calls? **To verify with counsel.**
- Do Do Not Disturb preferences apply to calls the patient asked for? **To verify with counsel.**
- Are there time-of-day limits that affect bedtime dose calls? **To verify with counsel.**

## Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020
- Does recording "I feel dizzy" and passing it to a nurse stay outside teleconsultation, as intended? **To verify with counsel.**
- Must the nurse callback follow the guidelines, and who is the registered practitioner for it? **To verify with counsel.**
- Could the halfway reminder ("Your doctor asked you to complete the course") be read as advice? **To verify with counsel.**

## Hospital data sharing agreements
- What agreement is needed between the hospital and the Care Taker operator to share discharge prescriptions? **To verify with counsel.**
- Where must data be stored and processed (in India, specific cloud regions)? **To verify with counsel.**
- Who is liable for a misunderstood reply or a missed urgent flag? **To verify with counsel.**
- What audit trail does the hospital need from the call logs? **To verify with counsel.**

## Optional third parties
- What do the Sarvam AI, Twilio and AI model terms say about health data, storage location and training use? **To verify with counsel.**
- Browser speech recognition may send audio to Google. Is that acceptable for patient replies, or must a server-side provider be used? **To verify with counsel.**
