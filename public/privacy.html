Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

1. What is collected

  • Patient name, age, phone number and phone type
  • Call language
  • Family contact name, relation and phone number, if given
  • Prescription schedule: medicine, dose, meal slots and number of days
  • Spoken replies, stored as text, and key presses
  • Timestamps of calls, replies and confirmed doses
  • Consent: what was agreed, when, and which nurse recorded it

2. Why

Only to follow up on doses after discharge: to call at dose times, record whether a dose was taken, and tell the care team when someone needs a person.

3. Consent and withdrawal

Calls start only after the patient agrees, in their language, and a nurse records that consent. A patient can stop the calls at any time by saying "stop calls" or pressing 9. The care team is then told, and no more calls are made.

4. Who sees what

  • The patient hears the calls and can ask the hospital for their record.
  • Family sees the dose record and the patient's words only if "Share with family" is turned on.
  • Nurses see patients, flags, replies and consent, to verify prescriptions and call back.
  • Doctors see course completion and flags with the patient's words.

5. Retention

The prototype keeps data only in the local file data/db.json on the machine that runs it. "Reset sample data" on the nurse board deletes it and restores the sample patients.

6. No selling, no advertising

Data is never sold and never used for advertising.

7. Children

The prototype does not handle children's data.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data. In the prototype, the hospital desk and nurse board do this for sample data.

9. Law

A real launch would be designed to comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and would be reviewed by a lawyer first. This prototype does not claim compliance today.
