Medical disclaimer

Care Taker reminds and records

Care Taker calls at dose times and writes down what the patient says. It does not diagnose, prescribe or change doses. Every decision about treatment belongs to the patient's own doctor.

Feeling better is not a reason to stop

If you feel better and want to stop a medicine early, ask your doctor first. When a patient says they stopped, Care Taker only asks why and tells the care team.

Feeling unwell

If a patient says they feel unwell or asks for a person, Care Taker raises an urgent flag so a nurse calls back. This is not an emergency line and nobody watches calls in real time. In an emergency call 112.

Sample data

Patients, medicines and doses in this prototype are made up. Medicine names are examples only and are not a recommendation.
