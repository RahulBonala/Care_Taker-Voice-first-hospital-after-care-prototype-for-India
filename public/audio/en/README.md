# English fallback clips

Used when the browser has no en-IN voice and no Sarvam key is set. Record each line below as `<lineId>.mp3` in this folder. Lines with {placeholders} are recorded with the most common value, and the screen always shows the exact text.

| lineId | Text |
|---|---|
| `greeting` | Hello {name}. This is Care Taker, calling from your hospital. |
| `askTaken` | Have you eaten? Have you taken your {slot} tablets? |
| `progress` | You are on day {n} of {total}. Your doctor asked you to complete the course. |
| `readback` | You took your {slot} tablets at {time}. Is that right? Say yes or press 1. |
| `confirmed` | Thank you. I have written it down. |
| `askAgain` | Sorry. Please tell me again. Have you taken your {slot} tablets? |
| `notTakenOffer` | Will you take it now? I can call back in 30 minutes. |
| `takeNow` | Please take it now. I will call back in {minutes} minutes to check. |
| `postponeOk` | Okay. I will call you again in {minutes} minutes. |
| `postponeLimit` | We have moved this dose three times. I am telling your care team. |
| `nurseComing` | A nurse will call you in a few minutes. |
| `askWhyStopped` | Can you tell me why you stopped? |
| `tellingTeam` | Thank you. I am telling your care team. |
| `askCallbackWhen` | When should I call back? |
| `unclear` | Sorry, I did not hear that clearly. Have you taken your {slot} tablets? |
| `keypad` | Please use the keypad. Press 1 if you have taken it. Press 2 to call later. Press 3 to talk to a nurse. |
| `noKey` | I did not get an answer. I am telling your care team. |
| `goodbye` | Take care. I will call again at {time}. |
| `goodbyeDone` | Take care. This was the last dose of your course. |
| `optOut` | All right. I will stop the calls. Your care team will be told. |
